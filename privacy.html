Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Ganeo AI ("we," "us," "our") is operated by Ganeo AI (sole proprietorship operated by Darshan Dangi). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit ganeoai.com or submit our opt-in form.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal and business information:

  • Name and business name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Your consent to receive text messages, including the date and time you gave it
  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, device and browser type)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for legitimate business purposes in connection with our website design and lead follow-up automation services:

  • To send you the preview of the website we built for your business
  • To answer your questions and follow up about our services
  • To send marketing text messages you agreed to receive
  • To operate and improve our website and services

3. Text Messaging (SMS)

If you check the consent box on our form agreeing to receive texts, we will text you at the number you provided. Up to 2 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile phone service plan. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or by emailing info@ganeoai.com. Reply HELP for help.

4. No Sharing of Mobile Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Service Providers & International Processing

We use service providers (for example, our messaging provider and website hosting) only to deliver our services. They may process your information on our behalf and may not use it for their own marketing. Because we operate from India, your information may be processed and stored in India or in other countries where our service provider infrastructure is hosted, with reasonable safeguards applied.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

7. Your Choices

You can opt out of texts (reply STOP), unsubscribe from emails (use the link in any email), or ask us to access or delete your information by emailing info@ganeoai.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top.
